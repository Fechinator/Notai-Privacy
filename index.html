3. Permissions and Data Processing in the App

In order for the app to function properly, it requires access to certain functions of your device. Below we explain which permissions are required and for what purpose they are used.

3.1. Microphone (Audio Recording)

Permissions:
android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO
android.permission.FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MICROPHONE

Purpose:
The app allows you to record voice notes. These audio recordings are processed and transcribed exclusively locally on your device. Only the resulting transcribed text (not the audio file itself) is sent to AI services for the creation of summaries (see Section 4.1 "Google Gemini / Firebase Vertex AI").

Legal Basis:
Art. 6 (1) lit. b GDPR (Performance of a contract) and Art. 6 (1) lit. a GDPR (Consent)

3.2. Calendar

Permissions:
android.permission.READ_CALENDAR
android.permission.WRITE_CALENDAR

Purpose:
The app can, upon request, read appointments from your calendar to link them with your notes, or create new appointments based on your notes. This function is optional and can be deactivated by you at any time in the device settings. The calendar data is processed exclusively locally on your device and is not passed on to our servers or third parties.

Legal Basis:
Art. 6 (1) lit. a GDPR (Consent)

3.3. Notifications and Alarms

Permissions:
android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS
android.permission.RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED

Purpose:
The app sends you reminders for your notes or appointments. The "Boot Completed" permission is used to restore scheduled reminders after a device restart. This functionality takes place exclusively locally on your device.

Legal Basis:
Art. 6 (1) lit. b GDPR (Performance of a contract)

4. Use of Third-Party Providers and Data Transfer

We use trusted third-party providers to provide certain functions. Below we inform you about the services used and the associated data processing.

4.1. Google Gemini / Firebase Vertex AI (Artificial Intelligence)

Purpose:
We use AI models from Google (Gemini/Vertex AI) to create AI-supported summaries of your notes.

Processed Data:
Only the text inputs created by you or the locally transcribed text of your voice notes are sent to Google servers for processing. Audio files are not transferred.

Data Transfer:
Data transmission takes place encrypted via HTTPS. Google processes this data in accordance with its own privacy policies primarily to provide the AI service.

Security Note:
We recommend not entering any particularly sensitive personal data (such as passwords, health data, or financial data) into notes that are to be processed with AI functions.

Legal Basis:
Art. 6 (1) lit. b GDPR (Performance of a contract) and Art. 6 (1) lit. a GDPR (Consent)

Further Information:
Google Privacy Policy

5. Local Data Storage

All notes, voice recordings, and settings created by you are stored primarily locally on your device in an encrypted database. We have no access to these locally stored data. You retain full control over your data and can delete it at any time by uninstalling the app or via the app settings.

6. Data Security

All data transmissions to third-party providers take place exclusively encrypted via HTTPS. We use technical and organizational measures to protect your data against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse.

7. Storage Duration

Locally stored data:

Stored indefinitely on your device until you actively delete it or uninstall the app.

Data at Third Parties:

  • Google Gemini/Vertex AI: Text inputs are used temporarily for processing and stored in accordance with Google guidelines.
  • RevenueCat: Purchase data is stored for the duration of the subscription and beyond in accordance with statutory retention periods.
  • Firebase: Crash logs and analytics data are stored in accordance with Firebase guidelines (usually 60-90 days).
